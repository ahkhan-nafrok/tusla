Mission-Driven Expansion
01
Accelerate Sustainable Energy Transition
The mission isn't profit — it's planetary transition. This framing attracts talent, investors, and customers who believe they're part of something bigger than a car purchase.
Mission Layer
02
Long-Term Bet on Full Autonomy
Tesla collects billions of real-world driving miles of data daily from its fleet. If FSD achieves L4–L5, the company transforms into a robotaxi network — a completely different, massively scalable business model.
10-Year Play
03
Energy Business Diversification
Megapack orders from utilities are accelerating globally. Tesla's energy storage division could eventually rival automotive revenue — and historically carries better margins.
Revenue Mix
04
Aggressive Cost Reduction via Manufacturing
Giga Press casting, 4680 battery cells, and single-piece underbody manufacturing reduce cost per vehicle by ~$2,000+. The goal: a $25K Tesla accessible to mainstream buyers globally.
Cost Moat
05
